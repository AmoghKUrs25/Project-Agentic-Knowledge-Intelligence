COMPANY KNOWLEDGE BASE

LEAVE POLICY

Employees receive 24 days of annual paid leave per year.

Employees who have completed more than two years of continuous service are eligible for an additional 5 days of service leave.

Employees must submit planned leave requests at least three working days before the intended leave date.

Emergency leave may be requested without the three-day advance requirement.

TRAINING POLICY

Employees can participate in technical training programs after completing six months of employment.

Employees with more than two years of experience are eligible for advanced leadership training.

Training requests must be approved by the employee's manager.

Employees can participate in machine learning, cloud computing, cybersecurity and software engineering training programs.

SECURITY POLICY

Employees must use strong passwords containing at least twelve characters.

Passwords must not be shared with other employees.

Multi-factor authentication is required for company systems containing confidential information.

Employees must report suspected security incidents to the security team immediately.

PROJECT POLICY

Machine learning projects may use Python, TensorFlow and PyTorch.

Cloud projects commonly use AWS services.

Cybersecurity projects require security review before deployment.

Software engineering projects must include automated testing before production deployment.

PROJECT RELATIONSHIPS

Machine learning is related to artificial intelligence and data science.

Cloud computing is related to distributed systems and machine learning infrastructure.

Cybersecurity is related to authentication, authorization and secure software development.

Training programs are related to employee development and technical skill improvement.
